## Synthetic session replay

`scripts/replay-session.ts` ran these eight existing fixtures with both its one-word and two-word
advance settings: manual correction, previous-ayah repeat, different-surah jumps, common opening,
longest ayah, silence, English commentary and the 55:13 refrain.

Each setting reported **65 measured display transitions, zero wrong displays and zero blank transitions**.
This is a bounded synthetic result; the script's wrong-display oracle checks membership among the
latest 12 truth words' ayahs, so it is not exact word timing or a universal accuracy claim.
Two reported flip-backs correspond to the requested repeat and return in `repeat-previous.json`.

Six highlight gaps were traced separately rather than called a clean highlight pass:

- One during a prior-ayah repeat, two during different-surah jumps, and one during English commentary
  occur while the last confirmed ayah is held without claiming an active recited word.
- Two occur at unsupported search-to-display mappings: word 80 of 2:282 and word 5 of 19:5. The
  mapper returns no span there. The source scripture remains visible; inventing a highlight would
  weaken the existing source-integrity boundary.

No replay timing is reported as new microphone-to-screen latency. These replays use the deterministic
session and do not measure live JEV or the scheduling repair's provider latency benefit.

## Focused Edge continuation

The owner approved “Run focused Edge browser checks.” Built the frontend and ran the installed Edge
against the real local server with placeholder provider keys, a disposable browser context, one worker,
and traces enabled. Existing user services and source work were preserved.

The first **12 checks passed in 40.1 s**, but reviewing the recovery trace exposed an intermediate
unstyled “Opening…” state on a white background. Final screenshots did not reveal it. The stylesheet's
body rules depend on `html[data-surface]`, which Reader and Control assigned in passive React effects.
The first render could therefore precede its surface styling. Two new first-content regressions failed
on the missing surface attribute before the repair.

`src/web/main.tsx` now chooses the page surface synchronously before creating/rendering the React root.
It preserves reader/controller styling and selects transparent audience surfaces separately. The saved
Paper theme continues to apply in its existing layout effect before the first painted app content.
`tests/ui/boot.spec.ts` observes the first committed opening state, checks its colors and font, delays
only the access response, and verifies the page becomes usable afterward. It covers Night, Paper and
controller startup without arbitrary sleeps.

After the repair, this command passed **15 checks in 40.8 s**:

```powershell
node node_modules/playwright/cli.js test boot.spec.ts reader-recovery.spec.ts walkthrough.spec.ts overlay-launch.spec.ts reader-continuity.spec.ts --grep 'first app content|connection failure|unavailable reader|failed surah|late surah|broadcaster|reading screen|reading accessibility|390px reading continuity' --trace=on
```

Night and Paper opening screenshots, audience short/long passages, hiding, lower-third page changes,
